How to play

  1. Move three lettersDrag one original letter from each of the first three rows into the word below
  2. Match all four cluesDrop at the marker to build each answer, then tap Check words
  3. Keep your three livesEach wrong check costs one life; three ends the round. Repeated guesses are free. Score = lives saved
Right spotWrong spotNot needed

After a wrong check, letters return to the start. Colors stay on each letter as a reminder of its last tested spot. Make three fresh moves to try again.

Moving letters
Any order. Drop outside to cancel. A new original letter replaces the move
The droplet
Slide a droplet letter within its row or return it up, never farther down
Keyboard
Enter: pick up/drop. Arrows: position. Backspace: return. Esc: cancel
Pool & sound
Tap pal: tilt/recenter. Note: mute all sound. Undo/Reset never refund lives
