WELCOME TO BUBBLE POST

Special delivery!

Turn each starting word into the answer to its clue. Send one original letter from each of the first three rows to the row below.

  1. Pick a letter. Tap an original letter in any of the first three rows.
  2. Pass it down. Tap a + slot in the next row to place it. The other letters stay in order.
  3. Follow all four clues. Complete three transfers, then check your relay. You get six checks.
Start anywhere—even at the bottom. An incoming letter must stay in its new row. Tap it to reposition it, or choose another original letter to revise a transfer.
