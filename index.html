LETTER RELAY

How to play

Turn each starting word into the answer to its clue. Send one original letter from each of the first three rows to the row below.

  1. Drag a letter down
    Move any original letter from one of the first three rows into the word below.
  2. Drop at the marker
    The line between letters shows the insertion point, and a preview shows the new word. Release outside the highlighted row to cancel.
  3. Check your words
    After three transfers, press Check relay. The daily relay gives you six checks; the optional hard relay gives you four. Green means the right letter and spot, yellow means the letter belongs elsewhere in the word, and gray means it is not needed.
You can make transfers in any order. A received letter has an envelope marker and must stay in its new row; drag it within that row to reposition it. Drag a different original letter down to replace a transfer.
