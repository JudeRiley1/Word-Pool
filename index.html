WORD POOL

How to play

Turn each starting word into the answer to its clue. Send one original letter from each of the first three rows to the row below.

  1. Drag a letter down
    Move any original letter from one of the first three rows into the word below.
  2. Drop at the marker
    The line between letters shows the insertion point, and a preview shows the new word. Release outside the highlighted row to cancel.
  3. Check your words
    After three transfers, press Check words. You start with three lives. Each incorrect check costs one life, even if several words are wrong. Three wrong checks end the round. Correct checks and repeated identical guesses cost no lives. Your score is the lives you save, out of three. Green means the right letter and spot, yellow means the letter belongs elsewhere in the word, and gray means it is not needed.
You can make transfers in any order. A received letter has an envelope marker. Drag it within its new row to reposition it, or back to its original row to cancel the transfer. It returns to its original spot. Drag a different original letter down to replace a transfer.

Keyboard: Tab to a letter and press Enter or Space to pick it up. Use Left and Right to choose a spot, then Enter to drop. Backspace returns a picked-up received letter to its original row. Escape cancels. On phones, scroll from the space outside the letter tiles.

Tap the platypus to enable phone tilt where permission is needed, or to recenter it around your current holding angle. You can also drag it.
